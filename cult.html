How to book

  1. Step 1Open the cult.fit app, go to Classes, and choose the Cult Andheri East centre.
  2. Step 2Pick Wednesday and a class. The plan suggests Boxing or HRX; Burn is a great fat-loss swap.
  3. Step 3Choose the slot closest to 5 PM. Evening classes run from 4 to 10 PM.
  4. Step 4Book both of you as early in the week as you can, because evening slots fill up. Check the app for the cancellation cut-off so you don't lose the booking.
  5. If fullBook another class at the same time, or swap days: do Legs at Step Up on Wednesday and take the class on Thursday.

For Momin's back

  • Tell the trainer about the back issue before class starts.
  • Brace your core on every rep. Rotate through the hips and feet, not the lower back.
  • Step out jumps and burpees if they hurt. Your pace matters more than matching the person next to you.
  • Stop if there's sharp pain or pain running down a leg.
