Diet

Training builds the muscle, but food decides whether fat comes off. These are starting targets. Weigh in every Monday and adjust after 2–3 weeks based on the trend.

Daily targets

Vikas · 80 kg Fat loss + muscle + strength (recomposition)
2,200–2,400kcal / day
140–160 gprotein / day
8,000+steps / day

Aim to lose about 0.25 kg a week while your lifts keep going up. If your weight stays flat for 3 weeks, cut about 150 kcal. If your lifts start dropping, add 150 kcal back.

Momin · 95 kg Fat loss + toning
2,200–2,400kcal / day
150–170 gprotein / day
10,000+steps / day

Aim to lose 0.5–0.75 kg a week. That's about 500 kcal below maintenance. Keep protein high so the weight lost is fat, not muscle. The daily steps and the 8-minute finishers do a lot of the work.

These are estimates from bodyweight alone; your heights and activity will shift them. The weekly weigh-in trend is the real test.

A workday, hour by hour

Built around training at 5 PM and a long commute home
8:00 AM
Breakfast · ~30 g protein3 eggs (or besan chilla with 50 g paneer) + fruit. Or poha/upma with a bowl of sprouts and curd.
11:00 AM
Mid-morning (optional)Chaas, a fruit, or a handful of roasted chana. Skip biscuits with your chai.
1:00 PM
Lunch · ~35–40 g protein2 roti + dal + sabzi + 120–150 g chicken, paneer, tofu or soya chunks. Add salad and curd. Rice only if you skip the roti.
4:00 PM
Pre-workout · light, 60 min beforeBanana + 1 scoop whey in water, or 2 boiled eggs + fruit, or 40 g roasted chana + chaas. Skip the office vada pav or samosa on gym days.
6:00 PM
Right after the gym · ~25–30 g proteinHave 1 scoop of whey (or 200 g hung curd, or a sprouts box) before you head home. With the commute home to Rabale (Vikas) or Bandra (Momin), dinner is 2+ hours away, so don't wait.
8:30 PM
Dinner · ~35–40 g proteinProtein first, plus a big portion of vegetables. Keep carbs moderate: 1–2 roti or ½ katori rice. Momin should lean to the lower end.
All day
Water & sleep3–4 L water (more in humid months). Aim for 7+ hours of sleep. With long commutes this is hard, but it has the biggest effect on fat loss and strength.

Two sample days

About 150 g protein each

Rules that make it work

Protein at every meal

Spread it over 4–5 meals of 25–40 g each, rather than one big dinner.

Watch the liquid calories

Sugary chai, cold coffee and juices add up fast. Have your tea with less sugar or none.

Weekends count

Two loose days can undo five good ones. Enjoy one treat meal, not a whole weekend of them.
