Guide

The rules behind the plan: when to add weight, how hard to push, how to protect a back, and what to do when life gets in the way.

Progression

Double progression

Each exercise has a rep range, such as 3 × 8–10.

  1. Start with a weight you can lift for the bottom of the range on every set.
  2. Each session, try to add a rep or two.
  3. When you hit the top of the range on every set, add weight next time: +2.5 kg on upper-body lifts and +5 kg on lower-body lifts.
  4. You'll drop back toward the bottom of the range. Build back up and repeat.

Deload & missed days

Every 6th week

Do the same exercises at about 60% of the weight and half the sets. You'll feel like you could do more, and that's the point. Joints and the nervous system recover, and the next block starts stronger.

Missed a session?

Shift the rest of the week forward and drop the last session. Never squeeze two workouts into Friday. If you miss Wednesday's class, take a 30-minute brisk walk instead.

Sick or wrecked?

Skip it. A missed day costs nothing, but training through illness can cost you a week. Come back at about 80% of your usual weights for the first session.

Back care for Momin

  • Use the swaps. Every heavy lift that loads the spine (back squat, RDL, deadlift, standing press) has a back-friendly alternative on the Workouts page.
  • Brace before every rep: breathe into your belly, tighten your core as if bracing for a punch, then move. Keep the spine neutral, with no rounding under load.
  • Avoid heavy loaded spinal flexion and twisting: sit-ups, Russian twists and heavy good mornings. Build the core with dead bugs, planks, side planks and Pallof presses.
  • Move daily. Long sitting at work and on trains stiffens the back. Get up every hour, and do cat–cow and a short walk at lunch.
  • Stop the set if there's sharp pain, pain running down a leg, or numbness. If it lasts more than a few days, see a physiotherapist before continuing to load it.

Recovery

Sleep

Aim for 7+ hours. Your commutes make this hard, so protect your bedtime. Strength and fat loss both stall without enough sleep.

Steps

8k steps a day for Vikas and 10k for Momin. Station walks and stairs count.

Weekends

No gym sessions. Take a long walk (Bandstand or Carter Road works) and do 10 minutes of the warm-up mobility at home.
