Gyms

Both are on your cult.fit membership and a short ride from the office in Andheri East. Step Up is a full gym for lifting; Cult runs trainer-led group classes.

Membership notes

Booking

Cult classes are booked through the cult.fit app, and the live timetable is only shown there. Step Up is gym-floor access, so check in at the gym. Check your plan in the app to see whether a booking is needed.

Before you rely on this

Hours and addresses come from the cult.fit listings as of November 2026. Centres sometimes change their timings, so check the app for the current details.
