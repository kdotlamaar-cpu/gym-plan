Why this layout

Every muscle, twice a week

Push and Pull hit each muscle group hard, and Friday's Upper day hits them all again with lighter weight. Hamstrings get RDLs on Pull and curls on Legs. Wednesday's class sits between Pull and Legs so you arrive at leg day with fresh legs.

Heavy, then dense

Each session opens with 1–2 heavy lifts for strength (6–8 reps, full rest). Then come supersets and drop sets for muscle, which fits 22–24 hard sets into 40 minutes.

A fat-burn finisher every day

Each lifting day ends with 8 minutes of intervals: incline walk, rower, bike sprints or a kettlebell / battle-rope EMOM. Together with Wednesday's class and daily steps, that does most of the fat-loss work.

The 6-week block

Week 1Find your working weights. Leave 2–3 reps in the tank.
Week 2Add reps within each range.
Week 3Add weight where you hit the top of the range.
Week 4Keep adding. Most sets end with 1–2 reps left.
Week 5Hardest week. Try to beat every logged number.
Week 6Deload: about 60% of the weight and half the sets.

After week 6, start a new block from week 1 using your week-5 weights as the starting point.
