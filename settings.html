Install on your phone

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open it from the home-screen icon. It runs full screen and works offline.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the home-screen icon.

Installing and offline use only work on the hosted site (GitHub Pages), not when you open the file from your computer.

Backup & sync

To share logs with your partner, export a backup and send the file (on WhatsApp, for example). They then tap Import backup. Importing replaces everything on that phone, so agree on whose phone holds the main copy.
